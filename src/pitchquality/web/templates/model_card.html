Model card

An expected-whiff model for pitch evaluation

The model estimates the probability that a swing misses from the physical properties of the pitch. It was trained on 2025 Statcast and evaluated on a 2026 season it never saw. The aim is to separate stuff, which travels with the pitcher, from results, which also carry the hitters he faced, where he located, and luck.

What this site shows

The 2026 MLB season is over. Nothing on this site scores live games. The fix that mattered

The first version of the stuff model scored {{ k.stuff_auc_before_vaa }} AUC and lost the predictive-validity test: a pitcher's own past whiff rate forecast his future whiff rate better than the model did. That is the right reason to reject a model, so the question became what it could not see.

The answer was approach angle. Statcast publishes trajectory as position, velocity, and acceleration 50 feet from the plate, so the angle the hitter sees has to be integrated forward. A flat vertical approach angle at the top of the zone misses bats that the same velocity and spin would not miss on a steeper plane. Adding vertical and horizontal approach angle moved the stuff model from {{ k.stuff_auc_before_vaa }} to {{ k.stuff_auc }} AUC.
