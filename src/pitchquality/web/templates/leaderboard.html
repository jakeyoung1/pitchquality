Stuff leaderboard

How often a swing should miss, judged from the pitch's shape alone: velocity, spin, movement, release, and approach angle. Each swing gets a stuff score where 100 is the average swing in the 2025 training data and 10 points is one standard deviation of those swings. A pitcher's score is the mean over every swing, so the board sits in a narrow band around 100.

Reading the table

Stuff
Mean stuff score, from the stuff model only. It is not the Stuff+ index in the research report, which is scaled across pitchers and spreads much wider.
xWhiff and Whiff
Expected and actual whiff rate per swing. Expected comes from the full model, which also sees location and count. A pitch nobody swings at is not counted.
Whiff over expected
Actual minus expected: the misses left over once stuff, location, and count are accounted for.

The database keeps every pitcher with a scored swing. Ranks and the default view start at {{ qualified_min }} swings, the floor the research report uses. A handful of swings can put anyone at the top.
